## Held up

- **No writes to the store.** In about 20 copy-path runs, including
  interrupted and concurrent ones, `changes.db`, the journal and all
  3,055 objects stayed byte-identical. `mode=ro` creates no journal or
  `-shm` file.
- **The write guards.** They cover Snapshot, Record, Restore, Replace,
  migrate and the M1 backup. scd cannot start on a read-only root (Init
  fails), so it never runs on a copy.
- **access(2).** It uses the real uid, honours ACLs, and gives EROFS for
  root on read-only and read-only-bind mounts.
- **The hot journal.** It is detected, the copy rolls back to the
  committed rows, and the store repairs itself on the next writable
  open.
- **Concurrency and privacy.** Eight concurrent readers each made and
  removed their own 0700 copy.
- **The texts.** They fit the rules table, and the film's emergency
  screen matches the lab's console log word for word.

## Found while fixing

- **SIGPIPE.** The harness's own shell ignores it (`SigIgn` bit 13), and
  sc keeps an inherited disposition. Re-raising SIGPIPE after cleanup
  does not end a Go program, because Go's default ignores a SIGPIPE
  sent by `kill`. So sc now exits 1 without a word in both cases, and
  the test runs sc both ways through `env --default-signal` and
  `--ignore-signal`.
- **A flaky watcher test.** `TestRestoreWhileWatching` failed once in
  the root pass, with a 5 s wait while every package ran at once. It
  passed 5 of 5 alone, and its wait is now 60 s, as `5b1d5a5` did for
  the other timing-bound tests.
