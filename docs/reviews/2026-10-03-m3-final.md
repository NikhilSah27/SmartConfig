# Final review of M3 (sign-off S4)

Date: 2026-10-03, after sign-off runs S1-S3 on the real system. Three
reviewers (AI agents), each in its own clone with its own `sc` build and
throwaway stores. The real scd, store and `/etc` were not touched:
validators ran as root only through `sc check --as` and `sudo unshare
--mount` probes that bind-mounted fabricated files in a private
namespace. The three areas were security and content leaks, checker
correctness against the real consumers, and operations (long runs,
bursts, sc edit and restore against scd, standing in for the soak cut
short). Claude reproduced each high and medium finding before fixing it.

**Gate: no high-severity finding open.** The checker reviewer found two
high ones; both are fixed (`297fbc7`). Six of the 7 medium findings
are fixed (`297fbc7`, `bb31f3e`), and so are 7 of the low ones and a
cleanup. The rest go on the M3 follow-up list in MILESTONES.md.

Two departures from the review rules, both read-only: the checker
reviewer ran `sudo grep -c` and `sudo wc -l` on the real sudoers files
(line counts only), and the security reviewer left one root-owned strace
output in its scratch directory. Claude deleted it unread.

## What held up

- **Runner.** The environment is only `PATH` and `LC_ALL=C`, with no
  `SUDO_*` or `SSH_AUTH_SOCK`. Tools come from fixed directories with no
  shell. Children in the tool's group are killed. Traced runs of the 11
  validators showed no network use, and no writes outside the scratch
  copy except the unit checker's man pages (now `--man=no`).
- **Leaks.** No Wi-Fi password, hash, fstab credential or sudoers token
  reached a finding's text or the journal, in about 20 fabricated files.
  No raw terminal escapes either. Fingerprint-only paths and checker
  globs do not overlap.
- **Checkers, against the real tools and glibc.** 185 fabricated files
  across 15 checkers: fstab (33), sudoers (13), sshd (20), units
  (17), grub (16), nsswitch (14), passwd and group (24), netplan, udev,
  sysctl and the plain rules. All behave as the consumers do, except for
  the findings below. The option-typo list flags none of about 400 real
  mount options. On 18 before/after pairs, `Added` never re-blames an old
  finding for inserted lines. With no validator, sc's own rules run with
  a note. All explanations are 2-5 lines of at most 76 columns.
- **Operations.**
  - Long run: 1,200 checks in 74 s on 40 flapping paths, a sixth of the
    validator runs timing out, killed or flooding output. Goroutines and
    file descriptors stayed flat, the heap grew from 0.7 to 1.4 MB, and
    no zombies or scratch directories were left.
  - Journal: one line per state flip.
  - Burst: 300 files recorded in under 0.5 s, and the worker was never
    held up.
  - SIGTERM mid-check: a clean stop.
  - Fuzzing: 19,200 mutated files and validator outputs, no panic.
  - `sc edit` racing a real watcher gave exactly the rows the plan
    promises.
  - No schema change, and `make m1-compat` passes.
- **Real system.** `sc check` as root finds no problems in 24 files, on
  the S2 build and on the fixed one.
