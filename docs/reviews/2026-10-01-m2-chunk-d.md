Date: 2026-10-01. Full review, as the plan asks for chunk D: three
reviewers (AI agents, each in its own clone), on concurrency, on event
handling and history, and on security as an unprivileged user against a
root watcher. Claude then reproduced each finding before fixing it.

## Not changed, with reasons

- A directory replaced by a symlink is "link first seen", not "did not
  exist": a directory did exist at that name, and a false "did not exist"
  row could delete it on restore.
- The unexplained race-mode failure noted in WORKLOG did not reproduce in
  any reviewer's runs (over 200 runs); the barrier weakness above is the
  most likely cause and is fixed.
- A new login's `~/.ssh` created after the passwd-triggered rescan is
  picked up by the hourly rescan (plan 7.5).
- Writes through higher directories in a restore follow paths by name; a
  dirfd-based restore is M4 (plan question 4).
