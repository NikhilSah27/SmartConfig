# Review of M4 chunk D (step 11)

Date: 2026-10-05, closed 2026-10-06. Two reviewers (AI agents) worked in their own
clones on `c7bc566..da706fd`. The chunk is:
- step 11, the QEMU rescue lab (`lab/`, `make lab-e2e`);
- its one product fix, `55d2bde` (`sc status --console` outlives the getty's hang-up).

Reviewer A took the scenario: `lab/e2e.py`, its checks and verdicts, `lab/test_e2e.py`
and the README. Reviewer B took the machinery and the Go fix: `lab/vm.py`,
`lab/serialmux.py`, `lab/console.py`, the guest scripts, the Makefile's lab targets,
`55d2bde` and its test. Neither started a QEMU: they replayed real console text from
the lab's runs through the lab's own functions, and drove the machinery with a
stand-in QEMU (a script serving the serial and QMP sockets).

They found 1 high (narrow), 10 medium, 13 low and 5 cleanups. None of it changes the
PASS of the step 11 check on `56f5359`: both runs had no retry, and every [M4] row
passed on its first attempt. What the findings say is that the lab, as a standing
proof, had holes: one check that passed a known product fault, verdict classes that
leaked both ways, retry paths and check conditions with no test.

## Open

- **The host's pauses.** This VM stands still for minutes at a time: 167 and 318 s in
  the UEFI run, 384 s in the first BIOS run, and 250, 364, 400, 400 and 265 s back to
  back in the second (24 minutes in all). They are the main cause of INCONCLUSIVE runs,
  and they made two of the review's faults show (3.4's lost byte, 2.5's lost report).
  The lab now names them (`mux.log`, `evidence/stall-*.txt`). They are the user's to
  look at on the host (sleep or power saving first).
- A follow-up idea for the lab: an ssh call whose timeout spans a host pause (a mux gap)
  could be retried once with a fresh budget, instead of ending the mode. Today it ends
  the mode as a lab error, which is right but costs a run.
- systemd's own status lines after guest time 126.9 in the BIOS boot 2 on `fc47ea3` are
  in the journal but not on ttyS0. Not understood; not needed for the fix.
- The mux's 1 s drain in `mark()` during a serial reconnect. `0ccba27` makes the
  SHUTDOWN safe; the drain itself still waits the whole second while the socket is gone.
- Load-bound tests: `test_the_first_signal_is_the_only_one` (from `9d3df9e`) hung its
  child past the 60 s limit in 3 of 20 runs with four CPU burners beside it, and once
  ended "SystemExit 143" with no teardown; the same script run directly under the same
  load had 0 hangs in 120. `test_the_loop_measures_its_wakes` (from `152ecbe`) failed
  once right after a `go test` loop; it expects the late wake to measure exactly 0.2 s.
  `make lab-test` alone passes, three runs in a row.
- One `go test ./cmd/sc/` of 14 failed right after three `lab-test` runs; its output was
  not kept, and 13 later runs passed.
- B14: `facts.sh`'s `p_blame`, `p_targets`, `p_dmesg`, `p_procs` and `p_vcs` report the
  last pipe command's status, and `p_consoles` that of `printf`, so a failing
  `systemd-analyze` or `systemctl` gives an empty block with `rc=0`. None of these
  blocks decides a check; they are evidence and dump output.
- B5: a SIGHUP before `signal.Notify` runs, in the first milliseconds of
  `sc status --console`, still ends sc by the kernel's default. It can only be closed
  outside sc.
- F14, one sentence: `lab/README.md` ("2.5 is a W wherever the serial text shows the
  shell returning, as in c") and the registry text in `lab/e2e.py` ("W in c") still
  describe the strength 9d3df9e removed. The code and its tests say F in every outcome.
- Item 6's second half: the main README's M4 section (step 13) should say that Ubuntu
  may start a login prompt on the emergency shell's console, and that the rescue entry
  gives a root shell nothing else reads from.

## Checked and found sound

- Retry bookkeeping for flakes before userspace: no `seen` line, the flag and the
  counts stay right, `check_30` restarts at the attempt's own mark.
- The stall gates: no observer line and no menu, or no `Run /init`. Reviewer A found
  no way for sc to produce either.
- 3.8.vcs1 is not self-fulfilling: `writeConsoles` fans out only when stdout is
  `/dev/console`, and in `facts.sh` it is a pipe.
- Expected values come from the guest (`sha256sum`), the boots file and Go's goldens,
  so 2.5 and 3.6 compare the real console with what the unit tests render.
- QEMU is never left running: `PR_SET_PDEATHSIG` kills it when the lab dies, SIGKILL
  included (8 of 8). The command line: TCG, no display, hostfwd on 127.0.0.1 only,
  sockets in the 0700 run directory. The cache 0700, the key 0600, the reference files
  0444 and renamed into place after `qemu-img check`.
- The Go fix `55d2bde` is right and narrow: `sc watch` is handled first, no other
  subcommand sets the flag, children are not affected (the signal is caught, not
  ignored, and exec resets it).
